Leave Policy for All Employees

Please be informed that the following leave policy will be applicable to all employees, including both junior and senior staff members:

Saturday or Monday Leave: If an employee takes uninformed or sudden leave on Saturday or Monday (the beginning or end of the week), Sunday will also be considered unpaid. In such cases, a total of 2 days will be unpaid.
Leave on Both Saturday and Monday: If an employee remains absent on both Saturday and Monday without prior information or approval, Sunday will also be included, resulting in 3 days of unpaid leave.
Emergency or Genuine Reasons: In case of a medical emergency, health issue, or any other urgent and unavoidable situation, the HR team will review the matter based on valid supporting documents and verification. If the reason is found to be genuine and approved by HR, the additional unpaid deduction for Saturday or Monday will not apply; however, Sunday will remain unpaid as per the applicable policy.

This policy is applicable equally to all junior and senior employees. Everyone is requested to inform HR in advance and obtain leave approval wherever possible. Any exception will be considered only after proper verification and approval by the HR team.

Leave Rules:

1. During Probation: No paid leave. Leaves will be treated as unpaid leave.

2. After Probation: 01 paid leave per month for medical, emergencies, or personal requirements.

3. Validity: Must be utilized within the calendar month. Cannot be carried forward.

4. Approval: Prior HR approval required. For emergencies, notify HR as soon as possible.
